    Response laws and behavioral mechanisms¶

    These functions evaluate model components. The application declares independent units, supplies compatible mechanisms, and chooses an estimator and query.

    Component

    Implemented scope

    Ranking and best-worst

    Strict top-k; ties up to order two; sequential or joint MaxDiff

    Subset choice

    Explicit enumeration of small feasible subsets

    MDCEV allocation

    Gamma profile, fixed budget, positive outside good, unit shock scale

    Consideration sets

    Exact mixture over supplied sets containing an outside alternative

    Correlated Probit

    Three alternatives; deterministic quadrature in a moderate-tail domain

    Continuous outcomes

    Gaussian intervals, censoring, truncation and correlated selection

    Response time

    Unit diffusion, fixed boundary/start/nondecision time; drift estimation

    Equilibrium mechanisms

    Finite-state interior rational inattention; contraction-domain mean field; two-player binary-action dynamic games on one regular branch

    Trajectories and supply

    Finite-horizon trajectory/causal laws; small interior Bertrand price roots

    Active-set changes and equilibrium selection require separate modeling work. MDCEV allocation queries differ from its observed mixed density. Global trajectory normalization and causal entropy use different laws under stochastic transitions.

    API reference¶

    event_log_probs(scores, available=None)¶

    Normalize arbitrary finite event scores, with an explicit feasibility mask.

    ranking_log_prob(scores, ranking, available=None)¶

    Plackett-Luce strict top-k. Unranked menu items remain competitors.

    ranking[…, stage] contains item indices, with optional trailing -1 padding. For rankings of a presented subset, declare that subset in available.

    tied_ranking_log_prob(scores, groups, log_tie=0.0, available=None)¶

    Davidson-Luce ordered groups, ties of order <=2, exact normalization.

    groups[…, stage, item] is boolean; empty trailing groups are padding. log_tie is log(delta_2); delta_1=1. An untied observation still includes tied outcomes in its denominator. Use ranking_log_prob for no-tie models.

    best_worst_log_prob(scores, best, worst, *, mechanism='sequential', available=None)¶

    Sequential best then worst, or joint MaxDiff pair normalization.

    subset_masks(n_items, *, min_size=1, max_size=None, device=None)¶

    Exact small-menu enumeration; caller owns constraints on feasible subsets.

    ponytail: exponential enumeration is intentional; use structured partitions for large sets instead of claiming general scalable combinatorial choice.

    mdcev_log_prob(utility, quantities, gamma, *, prices=1.0, budget=None)¶

    Mixed corner mass/face density, iid unit-scale Gumbel shocks, alpha=0.

    U = exp(V0+e0)*log(x0) + sum_i gamma_i*exp(Vi+ei)*log(1+xi/gamma_i). Quantities satisfy p*x=B with x0>0. Density is with respect to positive inside quantities, after eliminating outside consumption by the budget. Includes Gamma(number_active), unlike implementations omitting constants. Fix a utility location (e.g. V0=0); scale is fixed at one.

    mdcev_allocate(utility, gamma, budget, *, prices=1.0, shocks=None)¶

    Optimal quantities for specified shocks (zero shocks if omitted).

    This is a conditional allocation, not mean demand. Average explicitly over Gumbel draws for mean demand. Derivatives apply within a fixed active set; active-set switches are not twice differentiable.

    consideration_log_probs(utility, sets, set_logits, *, outside=0)¶

    Exact mixture over declared small consideration sets, with an outside good.

    sets[C,J] is a unique boolean support; logits[…,C] specify its distribution. Conditional choice is Logit. This is a parametric mixture, not nonparametric identification of attention. A set distribution may depend on observed data.

    class SetUtility(encoder, head)¶

    Permutation-equivariant scores from any shared encoder and scalar head.

    The head consumes [item embedding, masked mean menu embedding]. Network structure belongs to the application; inference/normalization remain separate.

    censored_normal_log_prob(value, mean, scale, *, lower=-inf, upper=inf)¶

    Two-sided censoring: endpoint atoms plus density in the open interval.

    truncated_normal_log_prob(value, mean, scale, *, lower=-inf, upper=inf)¶

    Density conditional on being strictly inside the declared observation interval.

    selection_normal_log_prob(selected, outcome, mean, scale, index, correlation)¶

    Joint Gaussian sample-selection likelihood, including unselected records.

    Selected iff index + e_selection > 0; e_selection and standardized outcome errors have unit variance and correlation rho. Unselected outcomes may be NaN.

    multinomial_probit_log_probs(utility, covariance, *, order=64)¶

    Three full-menu alternatives, correlated normal errors, fixed scale/location.

    utility[…,3], covariance[…,3,3]. User identifies the parameterization. A larger model can replace only this probability kernel, retaining estimation.

    ddm_log_prob(upper_choice, response_time, drift, *, boundary=1.0, start=0.5, nondecision=0.0)¶

    Unit diffusion, fixed a/z/nondecision time; trainable drift, no trial variability.

    upper_choice is boolean. Density is joint in choice and time, not a conditional RT density given choice. Image/eigen expansions use 21/32 terms after switching at t/a^2=0.12. Validated for .02<=z/a<=.98.

    rational_inattention(utility, prior, cost, *, tolerance=1e-10, max_iter=4000)¶

    Finite-state Shannon RI; strictly positive regular action support only.

    utility[state,action], prior[state], positive information cost. Returns (conditional policy, unconditional action probabilities). Boundary optima require support selection and are deliberately rejected by this interface.

    social_probabilities(index, network, interaction, **solver_options)¶

    Unique binary-logit mean-field fixed point; row-stochastic nonnegative network.

    abs(interaction)<4 guarantees contraction because sigmoid’<=1/4. This is an equilibrium mean-field model, not a joint distribution over correlated simultaneous realized actions.

    dynamic_game_values(payoffs, transition, discount, *, initial=None, **solver_options)¶

    Two-player, binary-action, finite-public-state dynamic Logit game.

    payoffs[2,S,2,2], transition[S,2,2,S]. Returns Q[2,S,2]. Private shocks are independent centered EV1. Computes one convergent regular branch from initial; no uniqueness claim, branch search, or NPL/EPL implementation.

    finite_horizon(reward, transition, *, terminal=None, mode='causal')¶

    reward[T,S,A], transition broadcastable to [T,S,A,S].

    causal: Q=r+E_P[V’]; transitions retain the physical law. trajectory: Q=r+log E_P[exp(V’)]; globally normalized trajectory utility tilts stochastic transitions too. They coincide for deterministic dynamics. Terminal values are rewards, and temperature is fixed at one.

    bertrand_markups(shares, prices, *parameters, ownership=None)¶

    Solve differentiated-product FOCs, holding supplied structural errors fixed.

    shares(prices,*parameters)->inside shares[J]. ownership[i,j]=1 iff the same firm owns products i,j. No constraints or global optimality certificate. Structural errors must not be re-inverted as prices are differentiated.

    bertrand_prices(shares, costs, initial, *parameters, ownership=None, **solver_options)¶

    One regular interior price-equilibrium root; user selects the starting branch.
