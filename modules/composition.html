    Joint responses and latent integration¶

    Use scoped factors when several responses share latent variables. A LogFactor records conditional log contributions together with their observation unit and latent-axis identities. A LatentAxis identifies a shared realization, not just a tensor dimension of a particular size.

    Order of computation¶

    1. Compute conditional probability or density factors.

    2. Join factors that share the same latent realization.

    3. Aggregate repeated observations within their declared parent.

    4. Integrate each latent variable after all factors depending on it are combined.

    5. Apply observation frequencies to completed observed contributions.

    join adds compatible log factors. sum_by aggregates conditional log contributions. Neither operation integrates a latent variable. integrate performs the declared marginalization. objective connects the completed contributions to estimation and can retain an explicit choice branch for queries.

    For permanent preferences, sum occasion log contributions before integrating that preference. Join one personal measurement only once. Prediction conditional on observed history requires updated preference weights; population marginal predictions use the declared population distribution.

    A complete example is available in the package at examples/composition/panel_density.py. The kernel contract and extension procedure are described in Build a new module with an agent.

    API reference¶

    class LatentAxis(name, scope='obs')¶

    Identity of a latent variable, shared within an observation or individual.

    class LogFactor(values, data, unit='obs', axes=(), _measures=(), _choices=<factory>)¶

    A log probability or density, with explicit unit and latent-axis layout.

    LogFactor also labels per-unit or multi-axis log masses/logits passed to integrate. Its numeric values are never choice-normalized.

    class ChoiceLogProb(values, data, axes=())¶

    Conditional log probabilities on long rows, separate from density factors.

    class LikelihoodObjective(units: torchdcm.composition.LogFactor, frequency: torch.Tensor, choice: torchdcm.composition.ChoiceLogProb | None, null_loglike: torch.Tensor | None = None)¶
    observe(choice)¶

    Select the observed choice, retaining the original prediction branch.

    join(*factors)¶

    Add conditional log factors at the same unit, aligning axes by identity.

    sum_by(factor, unit='person')¶

    Aggregate nested observations; local axes must have been eliminated.

    integrate(factor, axis, *, log_mass=None, logits=None, measure='probability', log_normalizer=None)¶

    Eliminate one latent axis at its declared scope.

    A 1-D tensor is global mass over this axis. For unit-specific or conditional multi-axis weights, supply a LogFactor with explicit unit and axes. logits are normalized on the eliminated axis. log_mass is checked, never rescaled. Raw importance mass requires measure=’importance’ and a scalar normalizer.

    objective(factor, *, choice=None, frequency=None, null_loglike=None)¶

    Finalize independent, unweighted units and retain their choice branch.
